**Learning:** A key BA learning was that confirmed requirements must be clearly separated from assumptions. Exact severity scales, accuracy thresholds, exposure limits, cooldown parameters and reassignment behaviour should not be invented before confirmation.

**Blocker / dependency:** Several business parameters remain pending confirmation. These are documented as open decisions so UX and Developers do not build unapproved rules.
